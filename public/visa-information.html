Visa Information

Important information for international travellers planning a visit to Nepal.

Check the latest requirements

Visa requirements, fees and entry procedures can change. Always verify the latest information with Nepal's Department of Immigration or Nepal Tourism Board before travelling.

Who Needs a Visa?

Most foreign nationals travelling to Nepal for tourism require a tourist visa. Indian nationals do not require a visa to enter Nepal. :contentReference[oaicite:3]{index=3}

Visa on Arrival

Tourist visas on arrival are available at designated international airports and entry points for eligible travellers. Nepal Tourism Board recommends having a valid passport and passport-size photograph and completing the required visa process. :contentReference[oaicite:4]{index=4}

What You May Need

  • A valid passport.
  • A passport-size photograph.
  • Payment for the applicable visa fee.
  • Any additional documents required for your nationality or travel circumstances.

Countries Requiring Prior Visa

Certain nationalities are not eligible for visa on arrival and are required to obtain a visa from a Nepalese diplomatic mission before travelling. The list can change, so check the latest official requirements before departure. :contentReference[oaicite:6]{index=6}

Indian Travellers

Indian nationals do not require a visa to enter Nepal. Nepal Tourism Board provides specific information regarding acceptable identity documents for Indian travellers, so travellers should verify the current requirements before departure. :contentReference[oaicite:7]{index=7}

Visa Extensions

Tourist visa extensions can be handled through Nepal's Department of Immigration. The applicable duration, fees and procedures should be confirmed directly with immigration authorities before applying. :contentReference[oaicite:8]{index=8}
