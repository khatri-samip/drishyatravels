Best Time to Visit Nepal

Nepal can be enjoyed throughout the year, but the best time depends on where you want to go and what you want to do.

Spring · March–May

Flowers & Mountains

Spring brings warmer conditions and blooming rhododendrons in many trekking regions. It is one of the best periods for trekking and mountain adventures.

Summer · June–August

Monsoon Nepal

The monsoon brings substantial rainfall to much of Nepal. Some trekking areas can become wet and more difficult, although rain-shadow destinations such as Mustang can offer different conditions.

Autumn · September–November

Clear Mountain Views

Autumn is one of Nepal's most popular travel periods. Conditions are generally favorable for trekking, with October and November often offering clear mountain views.

Winter · December–February

Crisp & Peaceful

Winter can be cold at higher elevations, but lower elevations and cities can still be enjoyable. This period can also be suitable for sightseeing and selected lower altitude journeys.

So, when should you visit?

For classic Himalayan trekking, spring and autumn are generally the safest starting points. For sightseeing, culture, wildlife and lower-altitude destinations, Nepal offers worthwhile experiences throughout much of the year. Always check current weather and destination-specific conditions before travelling.
