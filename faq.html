Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about travelling with Drishya Travels and exploring Nepal.

What types of trips does Drishya Travels offer?

We offer a range of travel experiences across Nepal, including trekking, adventure activities, cultural tours, sightseeing, wildlife experiences and customized trips.

How can I book a travel package?

You can explore our packages and contact us through WhatsApp, phone or email to discuss availability, requirements and booking arrangements.

Can I customize a package?

Yes. Many of our trips can be customized according to your preferred duration, destinations, activities, accommodation and travel requirements.

How many people can travel in one group?

Group sizes depend on the specific package and itinerary. Contact us with the number of travellers in your group and we will recommend suitable arrangements.

What is included in a travel package?

Package inclusions vary by trip. Depending on the package, they may include transportation, accommodation, meals, guides, permits and selected activities. Please check the individual package details before booking.

What is not included in a package?

Common exclusions may include international flights, personal expenses, travel insurance, visa fees and activities that are not specifically listed in the package inclusions.

What happens if I need to cancel my trip?

Cancellation and refund conditions depend on the package and booking arrangement. Please contact us as soon as possible if you need to cancel or change your trip.

Is travel insurance required?

We strongly recommend obtaining appropriate travel insurance before travelling, particularly for trekking, adventure activities and international travellers.

How can I contact Drishya Travels?

You can contact us by phone, email or WhatsApp. Our contact details are available in the website footer.
