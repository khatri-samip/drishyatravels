Before You Go

Travel Tips

A few practical things to know before exploring Nepal's mountains, cities, culture and wild places.

Pack for the Journey

Nepal's climate varies significantly with altitude and season. Pack according to your destination and activities.

  • Comfortable walking shoes are useful for sightseeing and city exploration.
  • Bring layers when travelling to mountainous regions.
  • Carry rain protection during the monsoon season.
  • Sunscreen, sunglasses and a reusable water bottle are useful for outdoor activities.

Money & Payments

Nepal uses the Nepalese Rupee (NPR). Cards are accepted at many hotels, restaurants and larger businesses, but cash remains useful, especially outside major cities.

  • Carry some Nepalese Rupees for smaller purchases.
  • ATMs are more readily available in major cities and tourist areas.
  • Keep your foreign exchange receipts when exchanging currency.

Altitude & Trekking

If your journey includes high-altitude trekking, allow enough time for acclimatization and avoid increasing altitude too quickly.

  • Stay hydrated.
  • Follow your guide's recommendations.
  • Take acclimatization days seriously.
  • Inform your travel company about relevant medical or fitness concerns before departure.

Respect Local Culture

Nepal has diverse cultures, religions and traditions. Travelling respectfully helps preserve the places and communities you visit.

  • Dress appropriately when visiting religious sites.
  • Ask before photographing people.
  • Remove shoes where local customs require it.
  • Respect local religious practices and monuments.

Stay Connected

Mobile connectivity is generally available in populated areas, while connectivity can become limited in remote trekking regions.

  • Download important maps before heading into remote areas.
  • Keep important documents backed up digitally.
  • Carry a power bank for longer journeys.

Travel Responsibly

Help keep Nepal's natural and cultural destinations beautiful for future generations.

  • Avoid unnecessary single-use plastics.
  • Dispose of waste properly.
  • Support local businesses and communities.
  • Follow rules in national parks and protected areas.
